# Competitor analysis (researched 21 October 2026)

Caution: most comparisons below come from AirDroid's own marketing pages. Treat them as vendor claims
and verify before using them publicly. Competitor prices were not independently verified.

## AirDroid Parental Control (main benchmark)
- Developer on Google Play: SAND STUDIO. 10M+ downloads, 4.5 stars, about 159K reviews.
- Parent app works from any OS; child app needs to be installed on each child device ("AirDroid Kids").
- Features: daily screen time limits, per-app and website time limits, downtime schedules, app blocking,
  live GPS location, geofences with alerts, 30-day route history, notification sync, website filtering,
  live screen mirroring, remote camera and one-way audio, activity reports.
- Anti-uninstall protection on Android and iOS child versions. Site advises parents to talk openly
  with the child before installing.
- Pricing (AirDroid comparison pages): $9.99 per month, about $6.66 per month quarterly,
  about $4.99 per month annual, or $59.99 per year. 3-day free trial of premium features.
- Awards or badges claimed: kidSAFE Seal listing, Family Choice Award, National Parenting Center seal.
- User complaint seen: iOS lacks the monitoring depth of Android.

## Life360
- Family location sharing. Per AirDroid: free plan covers basic location, no premium trial.
- Gap versus AirDroid (per AirDroid): no detailed screen time control.

## Find My Kids
- GPS and on-the-go safety focus. Per AirDroid: no remote camera or audio.

## iSharing
- Real-time location sharing with a privacy-first stance. 90-day location history (AirDroid keeps 30).

## Google Family Link (not researched in depth)
- First-party Google tool for Android; likely the largest free competitor. Review before launch.

## Where KidGuard can differentiate
1. Transparency by design: the child sees rules, time left and what is shared; no stealth mode.
2. Request-more-time flow so children can ask and parents answer in one tap.
3. India-first pricing (regional price points) and languages (English and Hindi first).
4. No covert surveillance features (no hidden remote camera or microphone), which also lowers
   Play Store review risk.
